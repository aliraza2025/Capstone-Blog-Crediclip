Deel Local XI: Turning Football Partnerships into Qualified B2B Meetings

An independent growth proposal for Deel. Deel already has Arsenal on the world stage; this concept tests whether one Bay Area community football club could connect that sporting energy with local employers who have a real Deel use case, measured in qualified meetings rather than impressions.

The pilot

  • Week 1, Qualify: research suitable community clubs and permission-based ways to reach relevant businesses.
  • Weeks 2–3, Design: with an approved club partner, plan a compact founders, HR, and operations event tied to a game or five-a-side tournament.
  • Weeks 3–5, Activate: invite well-matched businesses, run targeted invitations and local social assets, and offer a Deel conversation only when relevant.
  • Week 6, Decide: judge meeting quality, economics, opt-in interest, and qualified pipeline, then repeat, change, or stop.

Measurement & guardrails

  • North-star KPI: all-in cost per sales-accepted qualified meeting.
  • Where AI helps: find Deel-fit signals in public business data, score fit with human review before outreach, draft personalized creative, and report on qualified pipeline.
  • Guardrails: opt-in communication, quality over quantity, no outreach to youth participants, and no sponsorship unless the economics and club terms work.
  • Clubs to explore: San Francisco Glens SC and San Francisco City FC, both with published sponsorship information. No agreement or contact is assumed.

Independent concept, not affiliated with or endorsed by Deel, Arsenal FC, SF Glens SC, or SF City FC. Scorecard figures are illustrative assumptions, not results.

03 · Data Analytics

MrBeast: Predicting YouTube Engagement from Metadata

Can a creator predict engagement before uploading, using only things they control, such as title, length, and timing? This BAN 612 project tested that question on MrBeast's channel with interpretable machine learning.

Date
Fall 2025
Course
BAN 612 · Cal State East Bay
Data
250 uploads via YouTube Data API v3
Deck
11 slides
Python YouTube Data API Random Forest Gradient Boosting SHAP
250MrBeast videos analyzed
4models compared with 5-fold cross-validation
18–22hevening upload window that performed best

Method

  • Pipeline: API, cleaning, feature engineering, EDA, modeling, then SHAP interpretation.
  • Features: duration in seconds, publish hour and weekday, a peak-hour flag (18–22h), a Shorts flag, and title length.
  • EDA: views are heavy-tailed (so log-transformed), duration has a non-linear effect, and title length shows a small positive correlation.
  • Models: linear and logistic regression baselines against random forest and gradient boosting, scored with R² and ROC-AUC.

Findings

  • Performance: tree models modestly outperformed the linear baselines, and their bigger value was interpretability.
  • Top SHAP drivers: duration, the Shorts flag, publish hour, the peak-hour flag, and title length.
  • Local example: a 45-second Short posted at 19:00 with a balanced title was predicted high-engagement.
  • Recommendations: upload between 18 and 22h, run separate strategies for Shorts and long-form, and keep titles concise and keyword-rich.

San Francisco 49ers: Creative Content for a Global Faithful

A pitch deck I created as a prospective intern, proposing new content ideas to help the 49ers reach fans beyond the US. Each idea borrows a proven format from global soccer, cricket, and basketball and adapts it to the 49ers brand.

Date
Late 2024

Strategy

  • Why now: the 49ers gained international marketing rights in Mexico and the UK in December 2021 through the NFL's Global Markets Program.
  • Where to grow: the UK, Mexico, Spain, India, Germany, Saudi Arabia, and the UAE.
  • How to win: culturally tailored content, such as soccer-flavored stories for UK fans and Arabic-language appeal for Gulf audiences.
  • Formats: Reels and TikToks, drone POV, behind-the-scenes footage, player podcasts, and cultural music trends.

Content concepts

  • Players' Swag: arrival-fit content, inspired by France national team tunnel fashion.
  • Coach's Game Plan: a look at Kyle Shanahan's tactics, modeled on Pep Guardiola's locker-room content.
  • Player Carpool, Arabic commentary, and ASMR: formats proven by Arsenal FC and Cricket Australia.
  • Cultural moments: heritage celebrations like Manchester United's Diwali freestyle video.
  • Inside training: an "All or Nothing"-style series to win over UK audiences.
  • Levi's Stadium: tailgate and stadium-tour stories, including a Saudi-style desert tailgate idea.

Pitch deck prepared independently as a prospective intern; not an official 49ers campaign.
